🎓 Saint Joseph School of Fairview, Inc. – What We Offer
I. Educational Levels & Programs Offered

(Based on Section Three: Academic Framework)

SJSFI offers a complete K–12 program recognized by the Department of Education, with government accreditation for all levels:

Preschool (Kinder 1 and Kinder 2)
Focus: early literacy, numeracy, creativity, music, and values formation.
→ Subjects: Numeracy, Reading & Literacy, Natural Science, Music & Movement, Arts & Crafts, Values Formation.

Elementary Education (Grades 1–6)
Divided into:

Primary Level (Grades 1–3)
Focus: foundational literacy, numeracy, language development, and patriotism.
→ Subjects: Mathematics, Reading & Literacy, Language and Grammar, Makabansa, Science, Good Moral and Right Conduct (GMRC).

Intermediate Level (Grades 4–6)
Focus: deeper conceptual learning and practical life skills.
→ Subjects: Mathematics, English, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, Science, GMRC, HELE (Home Economics and Livelihood Education), Computer, Music and Arts, Physical Education and Health.

Junior High School (Grades 7–10)
Focus: competency-based curriculum integrating academic rigor and personal development.
→ Subjects: Mathematics, English, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, Science, Values Education, TLE (Technology and Livelihood Education), Computer, MAPEH (Music, Arts, PE, Health).

Senior High School (Grades 11–12)
Offers academic tracks aligned with the K–12 curriculum:

STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)
→ for aspiring scientists, engineers, researchers, and tech professionals.

ABM (Accountancy, Business, and Management)
→ for future entrepreneurs, accountants, and business leaders.

HUMSS (Humanities and Social Sciences)
→ for students interested in education, media, communication, or public service.

GAS (General Academic Strand)
→ for flexible learners exploring multiple disciplines.

Inclusive Education Program
Individualized education for learners with special needs or developmental differences.
Requires evaluation from a Developmental Pediatrician and relevant therapists.
Focus: inclusion, adaptive curriculum, and holistic growth.

II. Curriculum Philosophy & Framework

SJSFI follows the K–12 DepEd-approved curriculum, enhanced with the Josephian educational philosophy, which blends traditional structure and progressive innovation.

Core Principles

Holistic Development – growth of intellectual, emotional, physical, and moral faculties.

Learner-Centered Instruction – active engagement and collaboration.

Integrated Skill Development – cross-disciplinary literacy and real-world application.

Community Engagement – connecting classroom learning to social responsibility.

Inclusivity & Diversity – supporting all learners regardless of background or ability.

Faith & Values Integration – promoting the core Josephian values:
Excellence, Integrity, Dignity, Compassion, and Being a Person for Others.
